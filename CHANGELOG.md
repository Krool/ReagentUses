# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Hold Ctrl over a crafting material: its tooltip lists every recipe that uses it, grouped by
  profession, your professions first.
- Recipe names in the crafting window's colors for your skill; red while your skill is below the
  level the recipe is learned at.
- Shows who knows each recipe (you and your other characters), else where it is learned (Trainer,
  Vendor, Drop, Fishing, Quest, Recipe, Starter) and at what skill.
- How many of the material each recipe takes, and how many more recipes use what it makes.
- Settings: the key (Ctrl, Alt, Shift or always), the hint line, other characters, hiding grey
  recipes, and how many recipes to list. `/ru chars`, `/ru forget <name>`, `/ru errors`.
- Data from Forever build 1.60.1.70938: 2182 recipes using 526 materials. Vanilla trainer levels are
  moved by as much as Forever moved each recipe's thresholds.
