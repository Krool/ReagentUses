# Reagent Uses

> **Moved.** Since 0.19.0 Reagent Uses lives in
> [Krool World Forever](https://github.com/Krool/KroolWorldForever), and this repo is archived.
> It ships two ways from there: inside the Krool World Forever bundle, and on its own as
> **KW Reagent Uses** (same folder, `ReagentUses`, so an old install updates in place). Download
> `ReagentUses-<version>.zip` from the
> [latest release there](https://github.com/Krool/KroolWorldForever/releases/latest), or get it on
> [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/reagent-uses). The text below describes 0.1.0.

A World of Warcraft: Forever addon. Hold **Ctrl** over a crafting material and its tooltip lists
every recipe that uses it, grouped by profession.

```
Tailoring 152                                 1 recipe
  Bolt of Silk Cloth x4 (used in 95)             Known
First Aid 100                                2 recipes
  Heavy Silk Bandage x2                     Vendor 180
  Silk Bandage                        Known, Amy, Bob
Engineering                                  7 recipes
  Solid Dynamite (used in 1)                       Bob
  Iron Grenade                             Trainer 175
```

- **Your skill colors each recipe** the way the crafting window does: orange, yellow, green, grey.
  Red means your skill is still below the level the recipe is learned at.
- **The right side** says who knows the recipe (you, then your other characters). Otherwise it says
  where the recipe is learned (Trainer, Vendor, Drop, Fishing, Quest, Recipe, Starter) and at what
  skill.
- **Chains:** "x4" is how many of the material the recipe takes. "(used in 95)" means what the
  recipe makes goes into 95 more recipes, so Silk Cloth leads you to Bolt of Silk Cloth.
- **Order:** your own professions come first, then ones your other characters have, then the rest.
  A clicked chat link lists the recipes without the key.

Download: [latest release](https://github.com/Krool/ReagentUses/releases/latest). Unzip it into
`World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns`.

## Settings

Esc > Options > AddOns > Reagent Uses, or `/ru`.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show With | Ctrl | The key to hold: Ctrl, Alt, Shift, or Always Show. |
| Show Hint | on | While the key is up, one line says how many recipes use the material. |
| Show Other Characters | on | Names your other characters who know each recipe. |
| Hide Grey Recipes | off | Leaves out recipes that can no longer give you a skill point. |
| Recipes Shown | 15 | How many recipes the tooltip lists before it only counts the rest. |

`/ru chars` lists the characters it remembers. `/ru forget <name>` drops one. `/ru errors` lists
errors it caught.

**Other characters:** a character is remembered after it logs in and opens its own profession
window once with the addon on. Learning a recipe updates it right away.

## Where the data comes from

`Data.lua` is generated by `tools/data/gen-data.js` from two sources:

- **Forever's own client tables** for the current build, from [wago.tools](https://wago.tools):
  which recipes exist, what they use and make, their skill thresholds, and the recipe items that
  teach them.
  - That includes about 900 recipes Forever added that Vanilla never had.
  - It leaves out the Season of Discovery recipes the client still carries.
- **The [cmangos classic-db](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db)** for what the client does not
  hold:
  - trainer learn levels;
  - whether a recipe item is sold, dropped, fished or a quest reward.

  It is Vanilla data, so Forever's own numbers win where both exist. Recipe items Forever added
  show as "Recipe" until their source is known.

## Development

- `cd tools\test; npm install; npm test` runs the stub-client suite and the Forever API lint.
- After a new client build:
  1. Rebuild the allowlist with `tools\forever-api\build-allowlist.js`.
  2. Regenerate the data with `node tools\data\gen-data.js <build>`. The tests fail until both
     match.
- `scripts\link-addon.ps1` links the folder into the game for testing.
- `scripts\release.ps1 -Version x.y.z [-Publish]` releases.
